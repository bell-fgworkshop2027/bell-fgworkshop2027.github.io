Workshop Description

Large language models and multimodal foundation models are increasingly used for face recognition, facial attribute analysis, expression understanding and video-based reasoning. Yet their evaluation remains fragmented across datasets, protocols, metrics, prompts and model versions, which makes fair comparison difficult.

The 1st Workshop on Benchmarking Facial Image Analysis Technologies in the Age of Large Language Models (BELL) brings together researchers in face biometrics, facial analysis and multimodal AI to review existing benchmarks, identify their limitations, and promote dataset sharing, reproducible evaluation, and new benchmarks, tools and challenges for face analysis with foundation models.

BELL renews the BeFIT workshops held at ICCV 2011 and ECCV 2012 for the foundation-model era. Participation takes place in person during IEEE FG 2027, held in Marrakesh, Morocco, 26–30 April 2027. FG workshops take place on 26 or 30 April.

Call for Papers

We invite original research, benchmark and dataset proposals, and reproducibility studies on evaluating LLMs, MLLMs and VLMs for face analysis. Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • LLMs, MLLMs and VLMs for face understanding, verification, recognition and facial attributes
  • Facial expression, affective computing, multi-person analysis and video reasoning
  • Low-resolution, unconstrained and cross-spectrum settings
  • Metrics, operating points, calibration, uncertainty and comparison with specialist systems
  • Prompt sensitivity, in-context examples, decoding settings and model-version effects
  • Training/test contamination, benchmark leakage and data provenance
  • New datasets, benchmarks, protocols, evaluation harnesses, baselines and challenges
  • Well-documented negative results and reproducibility studies
  • Fairness and bias analysis
  • Privacy, consent, licensing and data governance
  • Security and robustness: adversarial, presentation and morphing attacks, deepfakes
  • Faithfulness of explanations for face-analysis decisions
  • Synthetic data, multimodal agents and tool-assisted face analysis
  • Ethics, regulation and revisiting classical face benchmarks

All submissions must address a measurable face analysis question. We encourage authors to report model and API versions, prompts, decoding settings, data provenance and licensing, metrics, and limitations.

Submission

Papers follow the FG 2027 paper format: short papers (4 pages + 1 page of references) or long papers (8 pages + references), using the official templates:

  • Overleaf LaTeX template
  • LaTeX template (zip)
  • Word template

Papers are submitted through CMT; select the BELL workshop track confirm track name once the workshop track opens.

  • Review: double-blind. Remove author and institution names and acknowledgments, and cite your own work in the third person.
  • Ethics: authors complete the FG 2027 ethics checklist at submission.
  • Originality: papers must not be under review elsewhere. Preprints on arXiv are not considered dual submission.
  • Proceedings: accepted papers are published in IEEE Xplore. At least one author must register and present the paper in person.
